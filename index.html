Choosing Suppliers Wisely

In New Hampshire, electric bills are divided into Supply and Delivery. Choosing a competitive supplier replaces only the supply portion of your bill. Your local utility will still deliver the power, maintain wires, restore service in outages, and bill you.

This dashboard exclusively tracks suppliers that feature $0 cancellation fees (no risk of being locked in if market rates fall) and no introductory pricing (ensuring your rate stays fixed for the full stated term instead of rising sharply after 2-3 months).
